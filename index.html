The person behind the work

My story

It starts with your customers

Your customers find you on their phones first — and if there is no website, they choose the place that has one.

Who I am

I'm Abdulla Al Hossain, a digital services specialist in Dhaka, Bangladesh. I work in English, with businesses worldwide.

Why I build first

I started the other way round. Rather than asking a café owner to trust a promise on a screen, I researched a business — its reviews, menu and street — built its website, and showed the owner the thing itself. It felt honest. I kept working that way.

How I work

My method, Demo-First, has three steps:

  1. I look at your business first — reviews, presence, what customers need to find.
  2. I build a demo you can open on your phone.
  3. You decide — if it is right, we agree the scope in writing.

What I believe

I'm one person, not an agency: you deal with me. I never promise rankings, leads or sales — no honest person can. Your accounts, files and assets stay yours.

Do not take my word for it

Take my word for nothing: the sites on this page are live — open one on your phone.

Message me about your business — I will look first, and show you what I would do.

I'm the person who reads a business's reviews before building anything — and I'm the person who replies when you message me.

Cà Phê Lơ Mơ

Vietnamese café · Zurich, Switzerland

The challenge. A well-liked Vietnamese café with no website of its own. Customers searching on their phones found social posts and listings, but no single place with the menu, opening hours and directions together.

What I did.

  • Built a one-page website around the café itself — its dishes, its room, its street in Zurich
  • Put the menu, hours and tap-to-call where a phone visitor finds them without hunting
  • Designed it from the café's own colours and character, not a template

The result. A complete front door where there was none — menu, hours and directions now one tap away.
